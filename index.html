Who we are

Research, engineering and strategy in one team

BEZ CRAFT is a centre of expertise dedicated to the research, development and application of innovative technologies in artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things and digital transformation.

We were founded to accompany companies and public administrations on the path of digital evolution, transforming the potential of emerging data and technologies into concrete, secure and sustainable solutions. Our approach integrates scientific research, industrial experience and strategic vision to deliver projects that combine innovation with measurable impact.

We work alongside our partners with a collaborative, result-oriented methodology that prioritises experimentation, speed and the measurability of the value generated.

Vision

To help build an ecosystem where innovation, technology and responsibility coexist in balance, generating lasting value for people, businesses and society.

We aspire to be a reference point in artificial intelligence and the Internet of Things, promoting ethical, sustainable innovation focused on real impact.

We imagine a future in which technology amplifies human capabilities, fosters better-informed decisions and accelerates the transition to a digital, connected and inclusive economy.

Services

What we do

From research and prototyping to production systems — delivered by one team so that the software, the data and the devices fit together.

  1. 01

    Innovation & research

    From strategic vision to realisation through applied research, ideation, rapid prototyping and guided experimentation.

  2. 02

    Artificial intelligence

    Machine learning, generative AI, computer vision and natural language processing — plus AI agents and intelligent automation that carry out multi-step tasks, handle documents and requests, and work across your existing systems.

  3. 03

    Internet of Things

    Sensors, gateways and platforms that collect real-time field data for monitoring, predictive maintenance and automation.

  4. 04

    Digitalisation

    Digitising business processes, IT infrastructure and user experience to enable data-driven, agile operations.

  5. 05

    Software, web & mobile

  9. 09

Our approach

Responsibility, excellence and impact

Three pillars guide every strategic and design choice we make — combining technology, research and ethical vision to create value that lasts.

Responsible innovation

Every project is born where research, creativity and responsibility meet. We put ethics, transparency and data security at the heart of technological development, creating AI and IoT solutions that improve people's lives and the competitiveness of organisations while respecting social and environmental values.

Technological excellence

Sound expertise is the engine of the company. Our work rests on research, experimentation and multidisciplinary skills — engineering, data science, design and strategy. Agile methods and industry standards ensure quality, reliability and scalability, turning technological complexity into concrete value.

Impact & sustainability

Every innovation must create real, measurable impact. We orient our work towards lasting economic, environmental and social value, designing AI and IoT as tools to optimise resources, reduce waste and support sustainable growth for businesses and communities.

How we work

  1. Understand

    We study your process, users and constraints, then agree scope, timeline and cost in writing.

  2. Design

    We define the system architecture and user flows and confirm them with you before building.

  3. Build & test

    Development in short cycles, with each increment tested and reviewed by your team.

  4. Deliver & support

    Deployment, training and documentation, followed by maintenance and support.
