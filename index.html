Services

What we do

Six areas of work, delivered by one team so that the software, the data and the devices fit together.

  1. 01

    Custom software

    Business systems built around how your organisation works — records, billing, inventory, operations and internal tools.

  2. 02

    Web & mobile applications

    Websites, web platforms and Android and iOS apps, designed for the networks and devices your users actually have.

  3. 03

    Data & AI

    Reporting, dashboards and analytics on your existing data, and applied machine learning where it measurably helps.

  4. 04

    IoT & connected devices

    Sensors and devices for monitoring and automation in agriculture, utilities, logistics and facilities, with the software to run them.

  5. 05

    Integrations

    Connecting your systems to mobile money and payment gateways, SMS and WhatsApp Business messaging, and accounting software.

  6. 06

    IT consultancy & support

    Technology assessments, system selection, project oversight and maintenance agreements that keep critical systems running.

Approach

How we work

A clear process with agreed scope, cost and timelines — and working software you can review at every stage.

  1. Understand

    We study your process, users and constraints, then agree scope, timeline and cost in writing.

  2. Design

    We define the system architecture and user flows and confirm them with you before building.

  3. Build & test

    Development in short cycles, with each increment tested and reviewed by your team.

  4. Deliver & support

    Deployment, training and documentation, followed by maintenance and support.

About BEZ CRAFT

BEZ CRAFT - SMC LTD is a technology company incorporated in Uganda and based in Entebbe. We work with organisations that depend on their systems to serve customers, manage operations and make decisions.

We favour practical, maintainable solutions over complexity, use proven open technologies, and treat security and data protection as requirements from the first day of a project.
